What every team gets

One schedule

Trainings, games and events with the venue, notes and a map link. Paste a whole season's fixture list in one go, or repeat events every week or fortnight.

"Who's coming?" answered

Players and parents answer Going, Maybe or Can't, and TIRA sends the reminders, so the coach doesn't chase.

Updates and team chat

Announcements that don't get lost, plus a team chat with replies, mentions, reactions and quick polls.

Team features each coach can switch on

Each one lives in Team settings, so every team uses only what suits it.

Share availability

Players, or parents on a kids' team, see who's coming: first names only. On for new adult teams.

Close availability

Close a game's answers early to find fill-ins in time. Anyone answering after that is marked late.

Volunteer roles

Scorer, umpire or referee, oranges, snacks: families sign up on each game.

Coordinate rides

Families offer seats or ask for a lift to a game, by area.

Record results

Scores, an MVP for each game, and a season record for the team.

Game day lineupCricket

Pick and publish the batting order and the captain, keeper and opening bowlers.

Fixtures from PlayHQClubs

For clubs on PlayHQ, games arrive in TIRA by themselves. Ask us to switch it on for your club.

It also works for groups that don't play games, such as rides, walks or working bees.

Built with children's privacy first

  • A child is registered with their first name and date of birth only. No surname, no photo.
  • Emergency contacts and medical notes are locked. Coaches see them only if a parent allows it, and every look is logged.
  • Parents can see, change or delete their child's details at any time, in the app's Privacy dashboard.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy.

Getting started takes about ten minutes

  1. The coach installs TIRA (search "TIRA Sports" in the App Store or Google Play, or go to tiraapp.nz/get) and creates the team.
  2. They share the team's join code with players and parents.
  3. Families join with the code, and parents register their child.

TIRA is free for teams, players and families.
