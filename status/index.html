If something isn't working

When TIRA has a problem, we post it here first, and the app shows the same notice at the top of the Schedule. We update this page as things change and say when it's fixed.

While TIRA is down, the app still shows your team's schedule as it was last loaded. Answers and messages need TIRA to be back up before they'll go through.

Something wrong that isn't listed here? Use Profile → Report a problem in the app, or email support@tiraapp.nz.
