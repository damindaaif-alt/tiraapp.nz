Everything your team needs each week

Built for volunteer coaches and busy whānau, not tech experts. Every team gets these three from the start.

The schedule

Trainings, games and events with the venue, notes and a map link. Paste a whole season's fixture list in one go, or repeat a training every week or fortnight.

Who's coming

Players and parents answer Going, Maybe or Can't with one tap. TIRA reminds anyone who hasn't answered, so the coach doesn't have to chase.

Updates and chat

Announcements that don't get buried, plus a team chat with replies, mentions, reactions and quick polls.

Switch on what suits your team

Each coach picks the extras their team uses in Team settings. Nothing to buy and nothing to unlock.

  • Works at a ground with no signal: TIRA shows what it saved the last time it was online.
  • Add an event by describing it, like "training Wednesday 4:30 at Truby King Park".
  • Also suits groups that don't play games, like rides, walks and working bees.

One app, whatever your part in the team

Coaches and managers

Less admin, more coaching.

  • See the headcount before you leave home
  • Send an update the whole team actually sees
  • Reach emergency details, when a parent allows it
  • Record results and the season's record

Parents and whānau

Every child's schedule in one place.

  • Answer for each child with one tap
  • Get a reminder before every event
  • Sort rides and volunteer jobs on the game
  • Control what coaches can see about your child

Players

Know when and where to turn up.

  • See the team's full schedule
  • Say if you're coming in seconds
  • Hear about changes straight away
  • Follow the season's results

Children's details stay with their parents

  • Coaches see a child's emergency contact and medical notes only if a parent turns it on, and every look is logged.
  • Medical notes are encrypted.
  • Parents can see, change or delete their child's details at any time in the app's Privacy dashboard.
  • No ads, and we never sell anyone's data.

Set up your team in about ten minutes

  1. The coach downloads TIRA and creates the team.Sign in with a code sent by email or text. There's no password to remember.
  2. They share the team's join code.Send it to players and parents in a text, an email or the old group chat.
  3. Families join with the code.Parents register their child with a first name and date of birth, and that's it.
