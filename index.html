🛡️ Family-safe

Built for families, safe by design

Parents register children — we only store a first name. Emergency contacts and medical notes are encrypted and access-logged so coaches can keep tamariki safe without compromising their privacy.

  • Children's last names never stored
  • Medical notes encrypted at rest
  • Every coach access to medical info is logged
  • Parents can view, edit, or delete at any time

Privacy-first

Your data stays yours

TIRA is built for NZ community sports — not for harvesting data. We collect only what's needed to run your team, store it securely, and give you full control to delete it any time.

Read our full Privacy Policy → How to delete your account → Get support →
🔒

Encrypted at rest

Medical notes are encrypted using Supabase Vault — never readable in plain text.

✋

You're in control

Access, correct, or delete your data any time — no hoops, no waiting, no questions asked.

👶

Children's data minimised

We only store a child's first name. Last names are never collected or stored.

🚫

Zero ads, zero selling

We don't sell your data, run ads, or share your info with anyone for marketing.
