TIRA

Your team, together

Become a TIRA partner. TIRA is a free app that helps community teams and groups in New Zealand and Australia organise games, practices and rides — no ads, no fees. It's run by a small team in Aotearoa, and local partners help keep it free.

Who you'll reach

Coaches, parents, players and volunteers from local community teams. Your logo appears on the Partners tab in the app, shown to people in your country (New Zealand or Australia). Tapping your logo opens your website.

Partner spots

Gold

Your logo at the top of the Partners tab, at the largest size.

Silver

Your logo in the middle section, at a medium size.

Bronze

Your logo in a grid with other local businesses.

Every spot links to your website, and you can add a short deal for TIRA members (for example 10% off team kits). Spots can run for a set period, such as a season.

A good fit for

  • Sports shops, cafés, physios and local services
  • Businesses that already sponsor a local club
  • Anyone who wants to back community sport and the families behind it

What we never do

Partners don't get anyone's personal details, and we never sell user data. There are no ads in team chats, schedules or notifications — partners appear on the Partners tab only. See our Privacy Policy.

Get in touch

Email us with your business name, your country and the spot you're interested in, and we'll get back to you with the details.
