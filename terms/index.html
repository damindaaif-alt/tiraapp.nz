TIRA

Your team, together

Last updated: 13 November 2026

These terms are the agreement between you and Aurodox Ltd, the New Zealand company that runs TIRA ("we", "us", "our"). By creating an account or using the TIRA app, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use TIRA.

The short version: TIRA helps volunteer coaches, parents and players run community teams. Be kind and honest in your team, look after children's information, and only add people who belong in your team. TIRA is free to use. Tips are optional and help keep it running. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account.

1. Who can use TIRA

  • You must be 16 or older to create your own TIRA account.
  • Children under 16 don't create their own accounts. A parent or guardian adds them to a team and manages their profile. See section 3 of our Privacy Policy.
  • If you're 16 or 17, check with your parent or guardian before you join.
  • If you add a child, you confirm you are their parent or legal guardian, or have their parent's or guardian's permission.

2. Your account

  • You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email or mobile. Keep your email and phone secure, because anyone who can read those codes can sign in as you.
  • Give us accurate details, and use your real name so your team knows who you are.
  • One person, one account. Don't share your account or sign in as someone else.
  • Tell us straight away at support@tiraapp.nz if you think someone else has used your account.

3. Using TIRA in your team

When you use team chat, announcements, events, polls, rides or any other part of TIRA, you agree not to:

  • bully, harass, threaten or abuse anyone, or post anything hateful, sexual or violent;
  • share anything that puts a child at risk, including photos, contact details or personal information about a child without their parent's or guardian's permission;
  • post anything illegal, or anything you don't have the right to share;
  • send spam, advertising or scams, or use TIRA for anything other than running a team or group;
  • pretend to be someone else, or join a team you don't belong to;
  • try to get into data you aren't meant to see, break or overload TIRA, or copy, scrape or reverse-engineer the app.

You're responsible for what you post. You keep ownership of it, and you give us permission to store it and show it to your team so TIRA can work. Team chat messages are deleted automatically 60 days after they're sent.

4. Coaches, managers and admins

If you run a team in TIRA, you also agree to:

  • only invite people who belong in your team, and remove people who leave;
  • use players' and children's information only to run the team, and never share it outside TIRA without permission;
  • respect parents' choices in the Privacy dashboard, for example whether you can see emergency contacts or medical notes;
  • follow your club's or school's own rules, including any safeguarding and child-protection policies.

TIRA is a tool to help you organise. Your club or school, not TIRA, is responsible for how the team is run and for the safety of the people in it.

5. Tips and sponsors

  • TIRA is free to use. Tips are voluntary and don't unlock any features.
  • Tips made in the app are paid through the App Store or Google Play, under their terms. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their refund rules.
  • TIRA may show logos of partners who sponsor TIRA. A sponsor's logo doesn't mean we recommend their products, and sponsors never receive your personal information.

6. When we can suspend or close accounts

We may remove content, or suspend or close an account, if it breaks these terms, puts anyone at risk (especially a child), or if the law requires us to. Where it's safe and reasonable, we'll tell you why and give you a chance to respond. A team's coach or admin can also remove people from their team.

You can stop using TIRA and delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. Our account deletion page explains what is deleted and what the team keeps.

  • We may change, add or remove features as TIRA grows.
