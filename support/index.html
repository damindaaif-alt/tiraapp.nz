Help and support

Kia ora! Most things can be sorted quickly. Find your question below, or get in touch.

New to TIRA?

The quick start guide gets you set up in about five minutes: signing in, registering your child and linking your account.

Is TIRA down?

Check tiraapp.nz/status. We post problems there first, and the app shows the same notice at the top of the Schedule.

Report a problem

The fastest way to reach us: in the app, go to Profile → Report a problem and tell us what's happening.

Common questions

No questions match that. Try another word, or email support@tiraapp.nz.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account. Your account is deleted straight away. Some team records other members rely on stay with the team without your name, and messages you sent are deleted automatically 60 days after they were sent. Our account deletion page shows exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

I didn't receive my verification code

SMS code: Check that your phone number is correct and has no spaces or extra digits. Wait 60 seconds and tap Resend code. If it still doesn't arrive, email us with your phone number and we'll look into it.

Email code: Check your spam or junk folder. Verification emails can land there. The code expires after 60 seconds; tap Resend code to get a fresh one.

I can't see my team after logging in

Ask your coach to check that your phone number matches exactly what's on the team roster. If you're a coach, try pulling down to refresh the Schedule tab.

Push notifications aren't working

Go to your device Settings → TIRA → Notifications and make sure notifications are enabled. Then open the app and check Profile to confirm your device is registered. If you signed out, sign back in: a signed-out phone doesn't get your notifications.

Why does my Schedule only show 5 events?

To keep it easy to read, the Schedule shows your next 5 events (the big Next up card counts as one). Tap Show more at the bottom of the list to see the rest. Show past events lists your 5 most recent past events.

Where is our season record, and how do I set the season?

The Season Card at the top of the Team tab shows your wins, draws and losses and your last 5 results. Coaches, managers and admins set the season's first and last month under Profile → Team settings (managers see this as Season dates). With no dates set, every game counts. Results appear once a coach opens a game and taps Record result. They can do this for up to 14 days after the game.

A game was stopped early. How do I record it?

Open the game, tap Record result and tick Game abandoned. The score is optional: enter the score when play stopped, or leave it blank, and use the note to say why (rain, injury, bad light). The game shows Abandoned and counts as an A in the Season Card. It counts as played, but its score isn't added to the season's goals.

Who can be MVP (player of the match)?

When recording a result, a coach can pick an MVP from the team's players and registered children. They get a ★ next to their name on the roster for the season. Children are always shown by first name only, and a child whose parent has hidden them from the roster can't be picked.

Who is included in the Going / Maybe / Can't count?

Players and registered children are always counted. Coaches, managers and admins are counted only on a team with no registered children: an adult team, where they usually play too. On a kids' team they're usually parent volunteers, so their own answers stay out of the count and a parent who is also the manager isn't counted on top of their child. Parents are never counted; their child's answer is what counts. Everyone's answers still show in the event's attendance list.

What reminders does TIRA send before an event?

3 days, 1 day and 2 hours before: an "Are you coming?" reminder, only to players who haven't answered and to parents of children who haven't answered. 1 hour before: a "starting soon" reminder to everyone who hasn't said Can't. Coaches, managers and admins get it only if they said Going or Maybe. If an event is moved, the reminders still to come are sent for the new time.

I'm a parent and the team's manager. Why didn't my answer count for my child?

Your answer at the top of the event is your attendance. To answer for your child, open the event and tap your child's name under Players attendance.

Where do I answer whether I'm coming?

Open the event and tap Going, Maybe or Can't. The Schedule shows whether you've answered: Your team is counting on you: Update your availability means you haven't yet, so tap the card to answer. Parents see the same for their children (Nearly there! when some have answered), and answer for each child on the event. Once you've answered, the card shows it, for example You're going.

My partner accepted the invite but can't see our team

The invite code links your partner to your child, but the team code is what adds the team to their app. Ask them to go to Profile, tap Join a team, enter the team code (it's on your Team tab) and choose Parent or guardian. When asked "Is your child on this team?", they tap Skip for now. Please don't register your child again, as that makes a second copy.

How do I reply to one message in the team chat?

Tap and hold the message, then tap Reply. Your reply shows a short quote of the original, and the person you replied to gets a notification. Tap a quote to jump back to the original message. Polls can't be replied to.

How do I mention someone in the team chat?

Type @ in the message box and tap a name from the list. The name shows in colour, and that person gets a notification saying you mentioned them. A message that mentions you has a green bar down its left side. Typing a name by hand without picking it from the list doesn't notify anyone.

Can I copy a message or an announcement?

Yes. Tap and hold a chat message or an announcement, then tap Copy. On some phones it says Copy or share and opens your phone's share menu: tap Copy there. Once you paste it into another app it's out of TIRA, so take care with children's names.

Can I say why an event is cancelled or postponed?

Yes. Coaches, managers and admins can add a short reason (up to 60 characters) when they cancel or postpone, like Ground closed after rain. The team sees it in the notification, on the event's card in the Schedule tab and on the event itself. Keep it to the venue or the weather, and please don't name a child. Setting the event back to Scheduled removes the reason.

Can I close availability early for a game?

Yes, if your team uses it. A coach or admin turns on Close availability in Team settings. New games then start with Allow closing availability already ticked (untick it on any game you don't want to close; games added earlier need ticking by hand). On the game, tap Close availability: people can still answer afterwards, but coaches see their answer marked late, so they know who answered on time. Families never see who is late. Reopen removes the late tags.

Who can see who's coming?

Coaches, managers and admins always can. Everyone else sees only their own answer (parents: their own child's) unless a coach, manager or admin turns on Share availability in Team settings. Then parents see every child's first name and answer, and on an adult team players see a Who's available list. Nobody else ever sees notes, ages or lift requests.

Why is Share availability already on for my new team?

New Senior, Masters and Mixed teams start with it on, so players can see who's coming. Junior teams start with it off. If a child is registered on a team that started with it on, TIRA turns it off by itself, so no child's name is shared unless a coach chooses to. You can change it any time in Team settings, and once you have, TIRA leaves your choice alone.

Can I use TIRA for a group that doesn't play games?

Yes. When you create the team, pick Activity group under the sports. It's for rides, walks, working bees and other groups that meet but don't play other teams. You can also turn off Record results in Team settings for any team. Events then default to Activity, there are no scores, season record or MVP, and TIRA doesn't send result reminders. Availability, reminders, messages, rides and volunteer roles all work as usual.

Can I add someone to call for an event?

Yes. Coaches, managers and admins can tick Add a contact when adding an event (it's saved on every event in a repeating series), or add one later from the Contact card on the event. Everyone on the team sees the name and number on the event and its Schedule card; on the event, tap the number to call. Only add someone who's happy for the team to see their number. It's deleted automatically 7 days after the event.

Where are TIRA's Terms of Use, and when did I agree to them?

Read them any time at tiraapp.nz/terms, or in the app at the bottom of the Profile tab. Everyone agrees once, when they sign in: the sign-in screen says “By continuing, you agree to TIRA's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy”, and there's no box to tick. You need to be 16 or older to have your own account; younger players are registered by a parent or guardian.

Can I add a whole season of games at once?

Yes. On Add event, tap the list icon beside Describe your event, then paste or type your games, one game per line, each with its date (for example Sat 22/11 9:30am vs Eastern FC @ Ngataringa Park (H) Round 2). TIRA shows every game before anything is added, never adds a game that's already on the schedule, and puts your note on each one. The team isn't sent a notification; the usual reminders go out before each game.

Can TIRA get our games from PlayHQ?

Yes, if your club runs its competitions on PlayHQ. TIRA support switches it on for your club. In the app, go to Team settings → Fixtures from PlayHQ and tap it to send us a request. Once it's on, a coach, manager or admin taps Find your team in PlayHQ and follows your PlayHQ team (both, if you play Saturday and Sunday), and TIRA adds your games and keeps them up to date: new times, grounds and cancellations come through on their own, and the team is told about changes. If you change a game's time, ground or status yourself, that game stops updating from PlayHQ and coaches are told when PlayHQ changes it.

How do I change my team's volunteer roles?

Coaches, managers and admins: go to Team settings → Volunteer roles. Your roles are numbered in the order events show them. Move one with the up and down arrows, remove one with ✕, or type a new one (for example Snack Duty) and tap Add. Changes save as you go, so just tap Done when you're finished. Reset to defaults puts back your sport's starting list. A team always keeps at least one role. To stop using them, turn Volunteer roles off.

Can I copy an event's details?

Yes. On the Schedule tab, press and hold any event card (the Next up card or one in the list). TIRA copies the title and opponent, the date and time, the place and the event's notes, ready to paste into a text or email. It never copies who's going, children's names or the lineup.

How do I pick and share the cricket team for a game?

Coaches, managers and admins of a cricket team: turn on Game day lineup in Team settings → Features. Then open a game and tap Game day lineup. TIRA suggests a batting order from who's going. Move players, mark the captain, keeper and opening bowlers, add a fill-in, and tap Publish. Families can see it in TIRA straight away. TIRA doesn't send a notification, so use Share to post a short message in an announcement or the team chat, or Copy to paste the team anywhere. For junior teams, TIRA's batting pods make sure every child gets turns at the top of the order; Season shows who's due a turn.

How do I know where my child is batting?

Once the coach publishes the lineup, open the game in the Schedule tab. You'll see a line for your child, such as Eli bats in the top order · captain, and the full batting order with first names only. Top order is batters 1 to 3, middle order 4 to 7 and lower order 8 onwards. Players who weren't picked for that game aren't listed.

My child is already on TIRA. Do I have to fill in their details again for a new team?

No. Join the new team, then open Register your child. Under Already on TIRA? tap your child, and TIRA fills in their name, date of birth, emergency contact and medical notes from their existing registration. Check them, then tick the consent boxes for the new team. Privacy settings start fresh for each team, so you choose what the new coaches can see. Later, when you change the emergency contact in the Privacy dashboard, you can update it on all their teams at once.

Can I use TIRA at a ground with no signal?

Yes. TIRA opens with what it saved the last time it was online: the schedule for every team you're in, each event's venue and notes, who's coming, updates and recent chat. A strip at the top shows you're offline. Tap the venue to open your maps app, or hold it to copy the address. Answering, chatting and signing in need signal, and TIRA tells you straight away. Tip: open TIRA before you leave home so it has the latest details.

What are the amber "to do" rows on a game?

Only coaches, managers and admins see them. On a game that hasn't started, anything not set yet (the contact, the game day lineup or notes for families) shows with a dashed amber edge and a ＋. Tap it to fix it, and it goes back to a normal row. Families never see these, and nothing is sent to anyone.
