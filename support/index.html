Common Questions

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Privacy Dashboard → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. You can also visit our account deletion page.

I didn't receive my verification code

Check that your phone number is correct and has no spaces or extra digits. Wait 60 seconds and tap Resend code. If it still doesn't arrive, email us with your phone number and we'll look into it.

I can't see my team after logging in

Ask your coach to check that your phone number matches exactly what's on the team roster. If you're a coach, try pulling down to refresh the Schedule tab.

Push notifications aren't working

Go to your device Settings → TIRA → Notifications and make sure notifications are enabled. Then open the app and check Profile to confirm your device is registered.
