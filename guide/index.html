Quick Start Guide

For parents, guardians and players. Get set up in about five minutes, step by step — no tech skills needed.

1

Create your account and sign in

No passwords, ever
  1. Open the TIRA app. If your coach gave you a code, tap Join a team with a code and enter it. We'll then ask if you're a player or a parent or guardian, so your coach knows who's who. Already set up? Tap I already have an account instead.
  2. Enter your email address (the easiest way) or your mobile number.
  3. We'll send you a code to type in: 8 digits by email, or 6 digits by text message. Type it in and you're signed in.
Tip: Signing in on a new phone? Just do these steps again. Everything in your account will still be there.
2

Register your child

Team tab, then Register my child. Or Profile tab, then My Children.
  1. Enter their first name and date of birth. That's all we need. No surname and no photo.
  2. If you'd like to add an emergency contact and medical notes, fill in the box. This is kept private and locked. Coaches can see it only if the toggle is ON — you'll find that under Profile tab, then Privacy Dashboard, and it's off until you turn it on.
  3. Tick the two boxes to confirm you're the parent or guardian and that you agree to the Privacy Policy, then tap Done.
3

Linking accounts: two different things

Guardian link and player link sound similar, but they are not the same
Both connect a TIRA account to a player's profile on the team. The difference is whose account it is.
Guardian link

For parents and guardians

Connects your account to your child's profile, so you can see their schedule and manage their privacy settings.

Joined with a team code? After you choose Parent or Guardian, we'll show you the team's players. Tap your child's name, then tap That's my child.

Got sent an invite instead? Go to Profile tab, then Enter invite code, and paste it in.

Player link

For players themselves, aged 15 and over

Connects your own TIRA account to the profile your coach already created for you.

When you join, tap your own name on the "Is that you?" screen, then tap That's me.

Can't find your name on either screen? That's fine — tap Skip for now. You can register or link later from the Profile tab.
TIP

Adding a second parent or guardian. On the Team tab, under your child's name, tap Add another guardian to invite a co-parent, grandparent or caregiver. They'll see the same schedule, can RSVP, and manage privacy too.

Still stuck on something? Head to Support — or in the app, go to Profile, then Report a problem.
